About Lithuania

Lithuania is a country brimming with natural beauty, from lush forests to serene lakes. Its vibrant capital, Vilnius, hosts a mix of Gothic, Renaissance, and Baroque architecture.

Located in northeastern Europe, Lithuania is the largest and southernmost Baltic state.

Home to 2.6 million people, with Vilnius celebrated for its UNESCO-listed Old Town.

A nation with deep historical roots, from the Grand Duchy to modern independence.

History

The story of Lithuania spans centuries, from pagan tribes to one of Europe’s most powerful duchies, to a resilient modern nation.

14th–16th centuries: Grand Duchy era, one of Europe’s largest states.

20th century: Soviet occupation and the 1990 independence movement.

Culture

Lithuania boasts a rich cultural tapestry: ancient folklore, vibrant festivals, and a thriving arts scene fueled by centuries-old traditions.

Lithuanian language: one of the oldest living Indo-European languages.

Basketball: national sport and cultural phenomenon.

Tourism

Whether you seek golden dunes, medieval castles, or urban nightlife, Lithuania delivers an unforgettable experience.

Curonian Spit: UNESCO World Heritage site with sand dunes.

Kernavė Archaeological Site: Lithuania’s medieval capital.
